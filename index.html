About

Build first, then polish relentlessly.

I'm Detrear Yaeger, the name I build under, and lead game developer and producer at PlaymatriX Studio, a creative studio in Jaipur, India. I hold a Bachelor of Technology and am completing an Advanced Diploma in Game Development this year.

I like the prototyping phase most: turning an idea into something playable as quickly as possible, then iterating until it feels right. I care just as much about the unglamorous parts, like performance on cheap phones, store review, age ratings and backups.

On the games side I work in Unity's C#, Unreal's Blueprints and Verse in UEFN. On the web side I build with Next.js and Astro, and I measure everything with the same tools Google uses.
