About

Build first, polish relentlessly.

I'm Detrear Yaeger, lead game developer and producer at PlaymatriX Studio, a creative studio in Jaipur. I hold a Bachelor of Technology and am completing an Advanced Diploma in Game Development this year.

I like the prototyping phase most: turning an idea into something playable as fast as possible, then iterating until it feels right. I've shipped a puzzle game to Google Play in ten languages, a sixteen player island to Fortnite, and websites that score at the top of Google's tests.

I'm at home in Unity's C#, Unreal's Blueprints and Verse in UEFN on the games side, and in modern web frameworks on the other. Whatever the project, I care about the same thing: that it ships, and that it works for the people using it.
