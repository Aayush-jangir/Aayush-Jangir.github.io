Mirror Reflection

Dot to dot with a laser. Tap the next star and the beam draws the line. Close the shape and the picture becomes stained glass, carved stone or neon, depending on the chapter.

Get it on Google Play Message me on LinkedIn

The version on Google Play today is the first release. The rebuilt game in this video is coming to the same page soon.

What is inside

Eight chaptersEach one made of something different: stained glass, stone, neon, brass, woodcut, lanterns, embroidery and the night sky.
Sky atlasEvery picture you finish is kept, and replays its reveal when you open it.
Daily puzzleA new picture to draw every day.
Endless modeMore to draw once the atlas is done.
