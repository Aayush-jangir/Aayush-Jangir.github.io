New Target

A bilingual website and print catalogue for a company in Qatar that designs, builds and installs exhibition stands, kiosks and fit-outs. Designed and built from scratch.

Visit newtarget.qa
RoleDesign, development and print
StackAstro, Cloudflare Workers
LanguagesEnglish and Arabic, right to left
StatusLive since September 2026

The problem

The company builds striking stands for major shows in Doha but had almost no presence online. Customers judge a fit-out company by how its own work is presented, so the website had to look as considered as the stands.

What I built

  • A speed budget set by measuring 55 competitor websites first, then beating them
  • 114 pages in English and Arabic, with 45 projects told through real photography
  • Arabic with its own typeface and full right to left layout, not a mirrored copy
  • A quote form that hands the brief straight to WhatsApp, so a request becomes a conversation immediately
  • Automated checks on every build: 112 pages at two screen sizes, menus, forms, language switch, speed and an iPhone engine
  • Search Console and a sitemap set up from day one

Print from the same source

From the same content I generated a 12 page company profile, a 37 page work catalogue covering 46 jobs in both languages, and the company's visiting cards with a contact QR code. Because print and web come from one source, they can never drift apart.
