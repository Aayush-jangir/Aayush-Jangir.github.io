The Scar

A sixteen player, four team arena island for Fortnite, built solo in UEFN and Verse for the Epic Games UEFN Student Bootcamp, and published in August 2026.

Play on Fortnite
RoleSolo: design, Verse code, level and HUD

The idea

The bootcamp asked for a complete island that could pass Epic's review and go live. I wanted a team mode where every match leaves a visible history, so you can read the fight just by looking at the arena.

Every elimination permanently plants a banner in the killer's team colour. The player with the most eliminations becomes the Juggernaut, and from then on the title only moves by taking it from whoever holds it. Hold it long enough and a trophy chamber opens that only the Juggernaut can enter.

What I built

  • Seven Verse manager modules, each owning one part of the game, written and debugged solo
  • A pre-allocated pool of 100 banner props that are recycled rather than spawned, so a busy match stays smooth
  • Title transfer that only happens on an elimination of the current holder, with clear on-screen prompts
  • A HUD rebuilt after playtests, so players always know who holds the title and how long until the chamber opens
  • Everything needed to publish: Epic content review and an IARC age rating

Results

16players per match, four teams
0.29%missed frames in Epic production telemetry

The island passed Epic's content review and age rating and is live on Fortnite today.

What I learned

Performance in UEFN is a design decision, not a fix at the end. Pooling the banners from the start is what kept the missed frame rate under a third of a percent with sixteen players. And a HUD is only right once real players have tried to read it in the middle of a fight.
